How do I enable banking via *99#? ONE-TIME
Before using OffPay, enable your bank on the *99# USSD channel. This only needs to be done once:
  1. Dial *99# from your phone's dialer
  2. Enter your bank name when prompted (e.g. SBI, HDFC)
  3. Follow on-screen prompts to link your bank account

See the official *99# guide for details.

How do I enable *99# in the BHIM app?
If you use BHIM, toggle on the *99# USSD service in BHIM settings. Done once, you're set:
  1. Open BHIM → tap your profile avatar (top-left)
  2. Scroll down → tap Settings
  3. Find "USSD service (*99#)" → toggle ON
Which carriers are supported?
Airtel, Vodafone (Vi), and BSNL — yes. Jio — no. That's a technical limitation on Jio's network, not OffPay. Jio simply doesn't route USSD calls for the *99# service.
How do I enable the Accessibility Service?
  1. Open phone Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps
  2. Find OffPay → toggle ON → confirm the dialog
  3. Come back to OffPay. The tile should show GRANTED
Can't enable accessibility? (Android 13+)
Since OffPay is sideloaded (not from Play Store), Android 13+ blocks restricted settings by default. Fix:
  1. Settings → Apps → OffPay
  2. Tap the ⋮ three dots (top right)
  3. "Allow restricted settings" → confirm with PIN
  4. Open OffPay → enable Accessibility

See this guide with screenshots.

What is *99# UPI?
*99# is India's USSD-based UPI service. It works without internet — you dial a code, your bank shows menus through USSD dialogs, and you reply to send money. Works on any phone with a cellular signal. OffPay automates that dialog so you don't have to type raw USSD codes.
What are the two modes?

Auto — Default. OffPay handles the carrier dialog automatically using the Accessibility Service. You stay in OffPay throughout.

Manual — Copies the UPI ID and opens the dialer. You enter the rest yourself. No accessibility permission needed.

How does the overlay work?
OffPay drives the carrier's USSD dialog automatically using Android's Accessibility Service. It reads what the carrier shows and types the appropriate replies. We never see your PIN — it's typed locally on your device, never stored, and never sent to any server.
Is my PIN safe?
Yes. Your PIN is held in volatile memory only, masked as •••• in any logs, cleared within 500ms of session end, never persisted, and never shared. The carrier-side handling is the same path BHIM and GPay use.
What if the payment fails?
OffPay surfaces the carrier's exact error message. If your bank isn't linked to *99#, we route you to setup instructions. A 25-second hard-cap timeout prevents stuck sessions.
Is OffPay a registered payment service?
No. OffPay is a side project by Lakshya and Harsh. It is NOT affiliated with NPCI, your bank, or any telecom carrier. Every transaction is processed by your bank through the same *99# infrastructure that powers BHIM — OffPay only automates the on-screen interaction.
What are the transaction limits?
NPCI caps *99# at ₹5,000 per transaction and ₹10,000 per day. These are network-level limits set by RBI/NPCI — not OffPay. Your carrier may charge up to ₹0.50 per USSD session per TRAI tariffs.
Is the source code auditable?
Yes. OffPay is fully open source under the MIT License. The complete Android source is on GitHub at laksh-ya/OffPayApp. You can verify every API call, permission, and data flow before installing.
