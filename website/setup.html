HOW IT WORKS

Type, tap, done.

In Auto mode, OffPay uses the Accessibility Service to read and respond to the carrier's USSD dialog. Your PIN goes to your carrier, not to us.

01

Type or scan

Enter a UPI ID + amount, or scan a merchant's QR code offline.

02

Enter your PIN

Six-digit UPI PIN entered on-screen. Auto mode fires on the sixth digit.

03

OffPay drives the dialog

The Accessibility Service reads the carrier's USSD prompts and types the replies. An overlay covers the carrier dialog.

04

Bank confirmation

Your carrier returns NPCI's reply — success, failure, or an exact error message. Saved to encrypted local history.

TWO MODES

Auto or manual.

DEFAULT

Auto

OffPay handles the carrier dialog start to finish using the Accessibility Service. Branded overlay, real-time progress. You stay in OffPay throughout.

FALLBACK

Manual

Copies the UPI ID, opens the dialer with the USSD code prefilled. You handle the rest. No accessibility permission needed.
